Course Fit — What Wins at the Blue Monster

7,739-yard bombers' paradise with water on 14 holes and two par-5s over 600 yards (likely 3-shotters for the field). Last six winners here were all bombers. Key categories: Driving Distance, SG: Tee-to-Green, Proximity from 200+, Scrambling, and bogey avoidance. Wind exposure is real and balls tend to plug in the bunkers. Course rewards aggressive ball-strikers, not specialist putters.
