Course Profile

Quail Hollow is one of the longest and most demanding courses on Tour — 7,583 yards, par 71, renovated through 2024 for major-caliber conditioning. The "Green Mile" (holes 16-17-18) is a career-ender: a par-4, par-3, par-4 closing stretch with water in play on all three. The course rewards elite ball-strikers who can hit long irons.

Bermuda rough, firm greens, large putting surfaces. Guys who spray it pay the price. This is not a birdie-fest — the best scores here are built on elite tee-to-green play, not hot putting weeks.
