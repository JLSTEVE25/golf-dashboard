How the team format works in our pool: Each pair of golfers plays as one team. You pick ONE player to get that team's earnings — OR "double-down" by picking BOTH players on the same team, which stacks 2× the team's earnings into your score. Format: Four-ball (best ball) Thu/Sat, Foursomes (alternate shot) Fri/Sun. Cut after 36 holes for top 33 teams.
Low-purse week — don't burn stars. $9.5M is the 2nd-smallest remaining purse. Winner's share of $1.37M per player is less than half of a Signature Event. Save McIlroy, Cantlay, JT, Cam Young, Spaun, Rahm for premium weeks (Miami, Truist, PGA, Memorial, US Open). This is a leverage-play week.
