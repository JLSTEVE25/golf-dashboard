🧠 Strategy Notes — Week 11

🏆
This is the most important week of the season so far. The Masters winner earns ~$3.96M — more than any single pick can earn on the regular schedule. Deploying McIlroy + DeChambeau here could swing Stemorbuclin’ from 18th to inside the top 8 in a single week. Do not hoard stars for a $9M event.
📌
Scheffler being burned creates involuntary differentiation. The pool will be heavily Scheffler-weighted (he’s +500 and teams with him available will use him). If anyone else wins — McIlroy, DeChambeau, Rahm, Schauffele — we gain ground on the Scheffler-heavy field. Being forced off the #1 favorite may actually be an advantage this week.
🟡
LIV deployment is the key decision. Deploy DeChambeau now, save Rahm for PGA Championship (Wk 16, $20M). Or deploy McIlroy + Schauffele and save both LIV bullets for PGA + US Open. Don’t burn both LIV names here unless the Masters is the only major you feel good about — there are three more $20M+ events coming.
📈
The gap is closeable. $3.27M back from 1st is a lot on a $9M week. It’s nothing at the Masters. Keep deploying stars at $20M+ events — the only path back is to swing big at premium purses. Weeks 11, 14, 15, 16, 21, 25 are the ones that matter.
⚠️
No more “safe floors” for a while. Weeks 9 and 10 produced four consecutive picks that either missed the cut or underperformed. The Masters is not the time for a “conservative” pairing. If you’re going to take a risk-adjusted approach to Augusta, the risk should be on the upside, not the downside.
